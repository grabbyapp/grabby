Questions

Is Grabby free?

Yes, completely free with no ads. If it saves you time, you can send a small tip from the Support page inside the app.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

Google doesn't allow apps that download videos from YouTube on the Play Store, so Grabby is shared directly. Android asks you once to allow the install. That's normal for apps from outside the store.

How do updates work?

When a new version is out, Grabby shows "Update available". Tap it, then tap Install. Your saved videos stay.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Grabby is for Android phones (Android 7.0 or newer).

Can I download any video?

Only download videos you own or have permission to save, and respect the creators.
